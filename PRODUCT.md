# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Designers, UX researchers, agencies and product teams (the app's own copy names freelance designers, independent researchers, startups and small agencies). They plan a study, collect notes by hand, and need to defend decisions to clients or stakeholders with evidence.

## Product Purpose
Yedara is a connected UX research workspace. It helps people plan research, understand users, analyse competitors, synthesise findings and identify opportunities, in one project, with evidence staying attached from raw observation to recommendation. Tagline: Know. Explore. Understand. Core message: Better insights. Stronger decisions.

## Positioning
The modules share one project brief and one chain of evidence: observations link to insights, insights and observations link to journey pain points, journey opportunities hand on to the Opportunity Analyzer. Nothing is generated or invented (no AI output today); personas are labelled research-backed / hypothesis / proto-persona; unknown matrix cells are research gaps, not weaknesses.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Live modules: Research Planner, Persona Builder, Competitor Analyzer, Research Synthesizer, Journey Mapper. Opportunity Analyzer is in development (journeys already hand opportunities on to it). Exports: PDF, Word and Markdown for planner, personas, competitor analyses and syntheses; no journey export yet. Text files (.txt .md .csv .tsv) are read in the browser and never uploaded. Auth is Supabase (email + password). There is NO billing, no plans table and no prices. Free is the only live plan. Pro does not exist yet: shown as in development, no price, with an enquiry route via /contact. Stack: Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind 4, Supabase, deployed on Vercel.

## Brand Commitments
Manrope. Primary orange #C74504, secondary orange #F28C38, charcoal #333333, background #FAF9F6. Logo: public/brand/yedara-logo.svg, LogoMark component. Tone: modern, minimal, professional SaaS.

## Evidence on Hand
No customers, testimonials, usage statistics, integrations, benchmarks or performance numbers exist and none may be invented. Product UI previews are authored mockups labelled as sample data. Tracking: no analytics or advertising cookies; only the Supabase session cookies (necessary) and a theme preference in localStorage. Legal entity name, address and contact email are not recorded; the contact form is the contact route.

## Product Principles
1. Evidence stays attached.
2. Say what is unknown instead of filling the gap.
3. Show the real product doing its job; never fabricate proof.
4. Marketing is public, separate from the app, and every link goes somewhere real.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive from mobile to desktop, reduced-motion respected.
